# How it sounds

Everything you write in their name starts from how they write: for the first two of each kind, their own words, tidied (`outbox.md`, "Their words first"); after that, drafts modelled on what they approved. It sounds like a capable person writing quickly and well, not like an AI and not like a cover-letter template. Use their CV's language (British or American English, per `preferences.md`).

## Emails and messages

- Short. A follow-up is 3 to 5 sentences; a reply answers the question in the first line.
- Specific: the role's exact title, one concrete thing about the company or the conversation.
- Warm and plain. "Thanks for getting back to me" not "I hope this email finds you well".
- One ask per email, stated clearly.
- Sign off with their first name.

## Letters

- 150 to 250 words, three or four short paragraphs.
- Open with why this role, in their words, not "I am writing to apply for…".
- One or two pieces of proof from the CV that match what the posting asks for most, with the numbers.
- Why this company: something real from the posting or their site, not flattery.
- Close with availability or a plain next step.

## Never

- "I am writing to express my interest", "I'm thrilled/excited/passionate", "I believe I would be a great fit", "fast-paced environment", "leverage", "synergy", "delve", "results-driven", "team player", "go-getter", "hit the ground running".
- Claims that aren't in their CV, preferences, answers file or the thread.
- Salary numbers, notice periods or start dates unless they're in `answers.md` or they said them for this job.
- Exclamation marks in a first email. Emoji.
- Long dashes in pairs as decoration; use commas and full stops.

## Before anything is shown or sent

1. Every fact traces to their files or the thread.
2. Names, titles and the company are spelt exactly as they appear in the posting.
3. Nothing from the "Never" list.
4. Read the lessons for this kind: `SELECT lesson FROM lessons WHERE kind = '<kind>' ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 10;`
