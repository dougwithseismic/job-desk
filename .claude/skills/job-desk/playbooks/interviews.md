# Interviews

## The prep pack

When an interview is booked, write `<folder>/interview-<n>.md` and set `interviews.prep` to it. Keep it to two pages:

1. **Logistics:** when (in their time zone), how long, where or which link, who.
2. **The company in five lines:** what it does, for whom, its size and stage, recent news, all from their site and reputable sources, with links.
3. **The role:** what they'll be judged on, from the posting.
4. **Their stories:** five or six stories from their CV in situation, task, action, result form, each mapped to a requirement. Only from their own experience; ask them for the details you don't have.
5. **Likely questions,** with notes on how they'd answer from their stories: the role's usual questions, plus "tell me about yourself", "why us", "why leaving", and the gaps the fit notes flagged.
6. **Questions to ask them:** three or four real ones.

Tell them it's ready and offer to send it to their email or open it.

## Practice

If they want, run a mock interview in the chat: one question at a time, short, honest feedback after each answer, a tougher follow-up now and then.

## Afterwards

Their debrief goes in `interviews.notes`; a `log` row (`kind = 'interview'`); then the thank-you (`follow-ups.md`).
