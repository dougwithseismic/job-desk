## Claude specifics

- **Gmail:** the claude.ai Gmail connector (Settings › Connectors › Gmail). It searches, reads, drafts, sends and replies; Claude asks before each send unless the candidate allows it. Its tool names vary by app version: look for tools with "gmail" in the name.
- **The browser:** Claude in Chrome (the extension, the candidate's own Chrome with their logins) for job boards, LinkedIn and application forms. In the Claude desktop app, the built-in browser pane works too, but the candidate's logins live in their Chrome. Upload the CV to a form with the extension's file upload tool.
- **Google Calendar** (optional connector): put interviews in the calendar when the candidate says so.
- Skills are in `.claude/skills/`. If they don't show up (this session started before the clone), read the SKILL.md files directly.
